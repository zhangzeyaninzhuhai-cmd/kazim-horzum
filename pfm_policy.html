Privacy Policy

PaperCardFold respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

Effective date: Feb 2026

1. Information We Collect

PaperCardFold does not require account registration and does not ask for names, addresses, phone numbers, or other personal details.

However, the app uses third-party services (listed below) that may automatically collect and process certain information, such as:

  • Device information (e.g., device model, operating system version)
  • App information (e.g., app version, language settings)
  • Network information (e.g., IP address, user agent)
  • Identifiers (e.g., AppsFlyer ID; and the Advertising Identifier/IDFA only if you allow tracking on iOS)
  • Approximate location (as permitted by your device settings)
  • Basic app activity for measurement (e.g., install/first launch and app opens/sessions)

2. Advertising

PaperCardFold displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect certain information to provide personalized or non-personalized ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

Learn more about Google’s practices here: Google Privacy Policy.

3. Analytics & Attribution (AppsFlyer)

PaperCardFold uses AppsFlyer to measure marketing performance and attribute installs/app opens to marketing sources (for example, which campaign an install came from).

AppsFlyer may process device and app information to provide attribution and analytics. Learn more here: AppsFlyer Privacy Policy.

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, we request permission via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt (typically on first launch).

  • If you allow tracking, third parties such as advertising and analytics partners may be able to access and use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to help measure ad performance and improve attribution.
  • If you do not allow tracking, we will still be able to show ads and measure basic performance using non-IDFA methods (where available).

You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. How We Use Information

We use information (directly or via our service providers) to:

  • Show and measure advertisements
  • Measure installs and app opens (attribution & analytics)
  • Maintain app functionality, troubleshoot issues, and prevent fraud/abuse

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • AppsFlyer (Attribution & Analytics)

7. Children’s Privacy

PaperCardFold does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any data handling related to ads and attribution follows our partners’ policies and applicable laws.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
